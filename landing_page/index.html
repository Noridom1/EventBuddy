Let’s get me into Teams ✨

Two ways to bring Event Buddy in

Add it to Teams once, then chat with it privately or drop it into any group chat. Pick your path below — every step is spelled out.

0

First, add Event Buddy to Teams · one time

Download eventbuddy.zip, then in the Teams client open Apps → Manage your apps → Upload an app → Upload a customized app and pick the ZIP. That registers Event Buddy on your account. Now choose how you want to use it. (If you don’t see “Upload a customized app”, ask IT to enable custom-app upload for your tenant.)

1

Open Event Buddy

In the top search bar type Event Buddy, or find it under the ••• (More apps) on the left rail, and select it.

2

Add it the first time

Click Add (or Open). Teams opens a private 1-on-1 chat with the bot — pinned to your chat list for next time.

3

Just type — no @mention

In a private chat you talk to it directly. Try “Create an event called Summer Offsite on July 12.”

4

You drive everything

In a private chat you act as the host: create events & their channels, focus on an event, remind members, list tasks, brainstorm, and generate reports.
